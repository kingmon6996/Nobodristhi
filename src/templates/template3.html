In Memoriam and Official Notices of Record

Eleanor Vance, 88

1935 - 2024

Eleanor Vance, a lifelong resident of the city and dedicated educator for over four decades, passed away peacefully on Thursday evening. Born in 1935, she began her teaching career at Lincoln High School in 1958, where she inspired countless students with her passion for literature and history. Mrs. Vance was known for her rigorous academic standards balanced by a deep, unwavering empathy for her students.

She is survived by her two sons, Robert and Thomas, and five grandchildren. A private service will be held for the immediate family at St. Jude's Chapel, followed by a public memorial next month at the community center she helped establish.

James Harrison, 72

1952 - 2024

James Harrison, former architect and avid local historian, passed away following a brief illness. He was instrumental in the preservation of several historic downtown buildings during the 1980s revitalization project. His firm, Harrison & Associates, designed the widely acclaimed City Library annex.

Dr. Arthur Pendelton, 91

1933 - 2024

Dr. Arthur Pendelton, a pioneering cardiologist who served as Chief of Medicine at General Hospital for twenty years, died Tuesday at his home. Dr. Pendelton advanced the field of non-invasive cardiac imaging in the region and published numerous papers that remain foundational texts in medical education.

Beyond his medical achievements, he was an accomplished cellist who performed with the local civic orchestra. Donations in his memory may be directed to the City Symphony Youth Program. He is survived by his wife of 60 years, Martha, and their three daughters.

Margaret Sterling, 64

1960 - 2024

Margaret Sterling, proprietor of Sterling Antiques on Main Street, died unexpectedly on Monday. Known for her encyclopedic knowledge of 19th-century ceramics and her sharp wit, she was a fixture of the downtown business community.

Dr. Patricia Hoffman, 79

1945 - 2024

Dr. Patricia Hoffman, a pioneering research scientist specializing in renewable energy, passed away Friday. She held numerous patents and was recognized internationally for her work in sustainable technology development. Survivors include her husband of 51 years and four children.

Robert 'Bob' Sullivan, 85

1939 - 2024

Robert Sullivan, beloved retired firefighter and volunteer coach, passed away peacefully. He served the city fire department for 32 years and touched countless lives through his dedication to youth athletics programs.
